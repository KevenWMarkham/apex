## What the Telco uniquely brings to CPG / Adult Beverage

1. **Compliance footprint already in place.** Telcos already operate under state-by-state regulatory frameworks (Wireless Bureau, state public utility commissions, etc.) — they understand 50-state-compliance discipline. Most adult-beverage startups underestimate this.
2. **Age-verification infrastructure.** The household vault already holds tokenised travel documents (passport / TSA Pre) for the Travel Channel; extending to liquor-purchase ID is a straightforward extension.
3. **Brand-neutral allocation routing.** No retailer-conflict-of-interest in choosing which licensed retailer holds the allocation for the customer.
4. **Cross-Channel cocktail orchestration.** Recipe + mixer / garnish / spirits split across Retail + Beverage Channels — no closed retailer can do this routing.
5. **The Telco bill is the lower-friction billing instrument.** Allocation purchases at the moment of drop need to clear in 60 seconds — card-on-file declines are a real problem; the Telco bill rails through directly.

## What this Channel does that nobody else can

- Cross-retailer allocation alerts (Pearson's drops Eagle Rare 17 today; tomorrow Hi-Time gets a William Larue Weller — Channel sees both)
- Reserved-hold mechanism via Sazerac-side partnership (today this doesn't exist anywhere)
- Tasting-event + distillery-tour booking coordinated with Travel Channel for out-of-state experiences
- Cellar inventory tracking across openings, recipes, replenishment
- Age-verified compliance enforced at every transaction without the customer re-uploading their ID each time
