**RASHMI:** Because the TOP-program-scope question is the question that breaks LA28 conversations for everyone who gets it wrong. Walk the frame. What is the Wasserman team protecting?

When a TOP-program brand shows up in the marketplace runtime, that category exclusivity has to be respected. So the platform-vendor positioning has to be utility-substrate. The platform itself does not consume sponsorship rights. The platform mediates orchestration. The Channel-anchored brands handle their own TOP-status obligations.

**RASHMI:** Say that one more time, because that is the line.

**KEVEN:** The platform itself does not consume sponsorship rights. The platform mediates orchestration. The Channel-anchored brands handle their own TOP-status obligations.

**RASHMI:** Which means what, concretely?

The Channel anchors that are TOP-program brands — Toyota Connected, Visa — bring their own TOP status with them; the runtime does not grant or imply it. **KEVEN:** The runtime can surface Olympic-themed Adaptive Cards — *the swimming finals are tonight at 7pm,* *your watch party reminder is set,* *venue transit guidance for tomorrow's event* — without consuming sponsorship rights, because those are utility experiences.
