**Date:** 2026-04-18

## Critical Repositioning Correction

**APEX is NOT a product Deloitte sells.** APEX is an internal Deloitte delivery
accelerator used to speed up deployment of agentic agents for clients on the
Microsoft technology platform (Fabric, Foundry, Copilot, MCP servers, Purview).

### What clients buy
- **Deloitte Agentic AI Delivery Services** (advisory, implementation, sustain, managed services)
- Microsoft platform licences (Fabric, Foundry, Copilot, MCP servers, Purview, Entra) directly from Microsoft

### What Deloitte uses internally
- **APEX** — the accelerator: semantic-model library, agent catalogue, MCP tool library, HITL framework, audit-row discipline, Wave 1/2/3 delivery methodology

### Client visibility of APEX
- **Invisible.** Client does not hear "APEX" in executive conversations, SOWs, proposals, or deliverables
- APEX is a Deloitte internal methodology name
- Sellers still learn APEX internally so they can describe WHAT Deloitte delivers (agents on the Microsoft stack) and WHY it's faster (APEX acceleration)

## Independence Compliance

- No mention of "APEX product sold to client"
- No mention of "client's APEX deployment" — use "client's agent fleet" or "client's Microsoft-stack agent deployment"
- Deloitte ↔ Microsoft language remains: "technology platform provider", "collaboration", "technology relationship" (never "partner/alliance/JV")
- Two-contract model preserved: client ↔ Microsoft + client ↔ Deloitte (APEX is internal)
- Store 100 narrative is illustrative; persona-based; no direct client-attributable claims

## Success Criteria

- Every reference to "APEX" in client-facing context reframed as Deloitte delivery services
- Sellers internally retain APEX methodology awareness
- Store 100 narrative provides canonical example of Deloitte-delivered agents in production
- Independence posture strengthened, not weakened, by the positioning clarity
