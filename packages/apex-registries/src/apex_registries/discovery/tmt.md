# TMT — Discovery Prompts

Discovery prompts for telecom / media / tech engagements.

## Triggering-event probes

1. "Trailing-quarter AHT / FCR / NPS in your largest contact center — and where's
   the biggest call-driver?"

2. "Subscriber churn rate trend, and which segment is the most painful — high-value
   tier, MVNO subset, B2B SMB?"

3. "Ad-tech / content monetization: trailing-quarter IVT rate? What was last
   quarter's settlement adjustment to advertisers?"

4. "Trust-and-safety review queue: median time from flag to disposition, and
   appeal-overturn rate?"

5. "Network ops: DORA-equivalent metrics (incident MTTR, change-fail rate, deploy
   frequency) — where's the biggest gap to industry top-quartile?"

## Architecture / data probes

6. "BSS stack (Amdocs, NetCracker, MATRIXX, custom)? Sprint 15 BSS adapter family
   assumes API + selective DB-mirroring."

7. "OSS / network-mgmt: ServiceNow + Salesforce on the front, but what's behind on
   the network side?"

8. "Content-platform stack — for ad-tech: SSP / DSP / DMP touch-points; for media:
   MAM / DAM / OTT-delivery."

9. "Subscriber data platform — Adobe / Salesforce CDP / Treasure Data / homegrown?"

## Audit / governance probes

10. "PCI scope — which environments, what's the trailing audit posture? Sprint 13
    baseline applies `payment-card` Purview tier."

11. "Content provenance / C2PA posture (media clients)? Sprint 13 classifications
    align."

12. "GDPR / CCPA / state-privacy posture for subscriber PII?"

## Commercial probes

13. "Where's value-share cleanest: IVT recovery, churn-save attribution, network-ops
    incident-cost reduction?"

14. "Wave-1 budget shape ($0.75-1.5M envelope, 8-12 weeks) for a contact center +
    one adjacent agent?"
