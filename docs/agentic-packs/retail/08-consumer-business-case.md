# 08 — Consumer Business Case (Retail Channel)

> _Draft — errand-chain time savings as wedge; pharmacy-as-eldercare-extension as the high-emotional-stakes hook; multi-retailer transparency as the trust differentiator._

## 1. Wedge — errand chaining

A typical US suburban household runs 4–6 errands per weekend across 3–5 stores. The Walmart Channel orchestrator collapses this into a single optimized route:

| Component | Saved per weekend |
|---|---|
| Planning + list-making time | 20–40 min |
| Drive-time between stores (optimized routing) | 15–30 min |
| In-store wait (pre-ordered pickup) | 30–60 min |
| Avoided "ran out of X mid-recipe" return trips | 0.5–1.5 trips |
| **Aggregate weekend time saved** | **65–130 min** |

A $7.99 / mo subscription against 65–130 min / week of saved time at $15/hr is a $4.20–8.40 / mo value floor. The Telco's pricing sits comfortably below.

## 2. The pharmacy-as-eldercare-extension hook

The cross-Channel wedge: a household with an aging parent (already on HOM-03 eldercare) gets RTL-02 pharmacy automatically integrated. Mom's medication refill due-date flows to the Walmart Pharmacy → ready-for-pickup notification → orchestrator chains it into the next planned Walmart visit. **No memory load on the adult child.**

This is the same emotional-value pattern as the IROPS-recovery wedge in the Travel Channel — high stakes, high stress, low-frequency-but-high-impact when handled well.

## 3. Multi-retailer transparency

When the orchestrator routes to Walmart, the customer sees:

> "Walmart Pickup, $89.40. *Costco has same items for $84.20 but adds 12 mi drive (not on chain). Target has 4 items in stock — $11.20 in this basket — adds 6 mi (on chain). Show details?*"

This is the **opposite** of a closed-garden retailer experience. The customer sees the price comparison, sees the trade-off, and the orchestrator makes the trade-off decision **with** the customer, not for them.

## 4. Adoption funnel

| Stage | Conversion envelope |
|---|---|
| Home Channel subscribers | 100% |
| Aware of Walmart Channel | 65–80% |
| Triggered to trial | 25–35% |
| Active at 90 days | 75–85% |
| Cross-attach to RTL-02 pharmacy (if eligible) | 30–50% |
| Cross-attach to RTL-03 auto care | 15–25% |
| Bundle upsell (Family + Walmart) | 40–55% within 6 months |

## 5. What kills the Channel

- **Walmart inventory misses.** If the orchestrator routes a household to Walmart for an out-of-stock item, customer extrapolates. Real-time inventory sync is critical.
- **Pharmacy compliance error.** A single PHI-leak or refill error damages trust irreparably.
- **Sponsored-placement abuse.** If the orchestrator's GM suggestions look like ads, the trust narrative dies.
