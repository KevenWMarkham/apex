# 06 — Strategic Partnership Map (Automobile Channel)

> Multi-anchor partnership map across the full vehicle ownership lifecycle. Three anchors — AutoNation, Cox Automotive, Progressive — cover the high-value transactional moments; an extensive bench fills coverage gaps.

## 1. The three anchors

### AutoNation

**Lifecycle stages owned:** AUT-02 purchase, AUT-05 aftermarket (parts via dealer parts dept), AUT-08 resale (trade-in side)

| AutoNation benefit | Telco benefit |
|---|---|
| Higher walk-in conversion via verified-intent pre-arrival customers | Per-purchase attribution fee (0.5–2% of vehicle price) |
| Pre-negotiated price reduces dealer-side haggling time → higher CSI | Dealer-network exclusivity for service routing in AutoNation territory |
| AutoNation Finance origination volume | Financing-attribution + servicing share |
| Trade-in pipeline from Telco households | Resale-channel attribution |
| Cross-brand inventory across 300+ stores | Coverage for any household across most US markets |

**Why AutoNation as anchor:** largest US dealer group, owns 230+ dealerships across ~30 brands, has AutoNation Finance + AutoNation Insurance under the same roof. Closest thing to a single full-lifecycle partner.

**Archetype:** B + C + D

### Cox Automotive (Kelley Blue Book / Autotrader / Manheim / vAuto / Dealer.com)

**Lifecycle stages owned:** AUT-01 discovery, AUT-08 valuation, dealer-side ops infrastructure

| Cox benefit | Telco benefit |
|---|---|
| Higher-engagement, higher-intent search traffic | KBB / Autotrader / CarGurus CPC + CPA referral revenue |
| Dealer.com / vAuto inventory feeds | Authoritative inventory for the Channel |
| KBB Instant Cash Offer high-intent funnel | Trade-in commerce attribution |
| Manheim wholesale data | Real-time market valuation |

**Why Cox as data-side anchor:** owns the dominant auto-data infrastructure in the US. Their data feeds make the Channel's discovery + valuation features possible.

**Archetype:** A + B + E

### Progressive

**Lifecycle stages owned:** AUT-04 insurance, Snapshot UBI, telematics-based outcome share

| Progressive benefit | Telco benefit |
|---|---|
| Verified-telematics underwriting from household vault | UBI premium-savings share with customer |
| Lower CAC on Snapshot enrolment | Per-policy commission |
| Lower loss-ratio on Telco-distributed cohort | Outcome-share on loss-ratio improvement |
| Multi-vehicle policy attach | Cross-sell into homeowner / renter (future Finance Channel) |

**Why Progressive as insurance anchor:** most mature consumer-telematics UBI program (Snapshot has 20+ years of UBI history). Most willing major insurer to operate as a Channel partner.

**Archetype:** B + D + E

## 10. The three platform-defining partnerships for Automobile

1. **AutoNation** — without a major dealer-group anchor, the purchase wedge has nowhere to land. AutoNation's 230+ stores + AutoNation Finance + AutoNation Insurance is the single broadest partner.
2. **Cox Automotive** — without KBB / Autotrader / Manheim data feeds, AUT-01 discovery and AUT-08 valuation lack credible market data. Cox's data infrastructure is dominant.
3. **Progressive** — without a UBI-anchor insurance carrier, AUT-04 is just another quote shopper. Progressive's Snapshot history makes the telematics-based outcome share defensible.

If any one of the three says no, the Channel weakens significantly. If two say no, the Channel is unviable. **Plan the BD sequence around these three first.**
