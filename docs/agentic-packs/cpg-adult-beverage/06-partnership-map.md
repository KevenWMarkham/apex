# 06 — Strategic Partnership Map (Beverage Channel)

## 1. Anchor — Sazerac

The most coveted allocation calendar in US bourbon. Sazerac wins by being the source-of-truth for its own allocations and by routing scarce product to engaged enthusiasts rather than scalpers.

**Archetype mix:** A (allocation data) + B (action commerce) + C (Sazerac House venue distribution) + E (industry-spec leadership)

| Sazerac benefit | Telco benefit |
|---|---|
| Reduced scalper-flipper activity | $4.99 / mo Channel subscription |
| Improved allocation distribution to enthusiasts | 5–10% take rate on routed orders |
| Brand-experience marketing to engaged audience | Per-allocation conversion attribution fee |
| Sazerac House + distillery-tour ticket volume | 10–15% booking commission |
| Data on which SKUs drive engagement (for production planning) | Premium consumer cohort for cross-Channel commerce |

## 2. Brand-owner bench

### Diageo

- Single-malt + premium-spirits portfolio (Johnnie Walker, Crown Royal, Bulleit, Don Julio, Lagavulin, Talisker)
- **Diageo Special Releases** — annual single-cask + limited single-malts — allocation analog to BTAC
- Strong international portfolio (handoff for EU expansion)

### Pernod Ricard

- Irish whiskey (Jameson), Scotch (Glenlivet, Chivas), Vodka (Absolut), Gin (Beefeater)
- The Glenlivet single-cask + Aberlour A'bunadh allocations

### Brown-Forman

- Bourbon (Woodford Reserve, Old Forester) + Tennessee (Jack Daniel's)
- **Old Forester Birthday Bourbon** — annual allocation, cult following
- Woodford Master's Collection — annual limited releases

### Constellation Brands

- Tequila (Casa Noble, Mi Campo), Bourbon (High West), wine (Robert Mondavi, Kim Crawford)
- Growing premium-spirits portfolio

## 3. Retailer partnerships

| Type | Examples | Role |
|---|---|---|
| National chain | Total Wine & More, BevMo!, Binny's (Chicago) | Allocation drop venues; inventory feeds |
| Regional independent | Pearson's (DC), Astor Wines (NYC), Hi-Time (Costa Mesa), Bourbon & Banter | Source-of-truth for local allocation; high enthusiast brand affinity |
| Membership-club | Costco wine + spirits, Sam's Club | Cross-Channel routing via Walmart / Sam's |
| Direct-from-distillery | Sazerac House gift shop, Buffalo Trace gift shop, Heaven Hill | Where state-legal, direct-ship; otherwise on-site sales tied to distillery tours |

## 4. Age-verification partnerships

| Partner | Role |
|---|---|
| Veratad | Identity + age verification at order time |
| AgeChecker.net | Same; specializes in age-restricted commerce |
| BlueCheck | Same |
| Persona / Stripe Identity | Document-verification rails (handoff from existing Telco identity infrastructure) |

## 5. State / compliance partnerships

| Resource | Role |
|---|---|
| Alcohol Beverage Control (ABC) regulators per state | Authoritative state rules |
| Avalara for Beverage Alcohol | Tax + compliance rules engine — partnership ideal here |
| NABCA (National Alcohol Beverage Control Association) | Industry data + rule clarity |
| TTB | Federal compliance + labelling |

## 6. The three platform-defining partnerships for Beverage

1. **Sazerac** — anchor, justified above
2. **A compliance / state-rules rules-engine partner** (Avalara Beverage Alcohol or equivalent) — without this, the Channel cannot operate across 50 states
3. **A national age-verification provider** (Veratad / BlueCheck / equivalent) — without this, the Channel cannot scale beyond one-off ID checks

## 7. Phasing

| Phase | Quarter | Partners |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 0 | Year 2 Q3 — bourbon-states-only launch (KY, TN) | Sazerac + Avalara + one age-verifier |
| Phase 1 | Year 2 Q4 | Expand to ~25 DTC-friendly states; add Diageo, Brown-Forman |
| Phase 2 | Year 3 | Full 50-state coverage including 3-tier compliance; add Pernod Ricard, Constellation |
| Phase 3 | Year 3+ | International (EU spirits markets first; APAC second) |
