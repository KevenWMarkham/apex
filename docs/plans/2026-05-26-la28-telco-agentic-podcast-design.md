## Content discipline

Carries from the DTNA show bible and the CFMP narrative re-tone discipline, with LA28-specific additions:

### Independence language (non-negotiable)
*"Microsoft platform engagement"* — never *"alliance,"* *"strategic partnership,"* *"channel partner,"* or *"co-sell."* Deloitte audits Microsoft; the language reflects that auditor-independent posture. Repeated through every episode.

### LA28 / IOC brand discipline
The IOC has tight trademark restrictions on Games marks. The podcast names *LA28 · the Games · the Olympic period · the Opening Ceremony · the Closing Ceremony · the Olympic Rings* with care. The TOP-program scope boundaries are clarified in Episode 02 and respected throughout. NBCUniversal's broadcast rights conversation is named explicitly where it shapes a Channel decision (Media Channel scope in particular).

### Anchor-partner naming
Real entities — **AT&T · LA28 · NBCUniversal · Walmart · American Airlines · Marriott · Expedia · Airbnb · Hertz · Uber · OpenTable · Viator · Toyota Connected · AutoNation · Progressive · Sazerac · CVS · LA Mayor's office · CA Governor's office** — appear as themselves. Internal C-suite is referenced by **title and role**, not invented names (per the DTNA Rule 1).

### Narrative tone (from the CFMP re-tone discipline)
Every sub-section opens with a concrete pursuit moment before any framework term enters. Technical vocabulary tucks into asides. The seller's experience drives the close — what the TMT VP does Monday, what the Account SE briefs the partner with on Friday, what the delivery architect ships next.

### Quote-and-react discipline (from DTNA show bible)
One quote-and-react moment per episode, drawn from a primary source — AT&T earnings transcript, NBCUniversal Olympics commitments, LA28 organizing committee public statements, Microsoft Azure architecture-center reference patterns, Walmart investor day, etc.

### The 9 Practices × 11 Channels matrix
Every episode names the cross-Practice coordination. The 9 Practices (TMT · TH · RC · AXLE · ER · HLS · ICE · FSI · GPS) and 11 Channels (Home · Travel · Retail · Mobility · Automobile · CPG · Energy · Health · Industrial · Finance · Public) form the implicit coordination grid. The tracker's `practice-strip` and per-Channel accent palette are reflected in episode framing.
