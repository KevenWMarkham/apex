Act 1 · Why now

The transportation operations gap is widening.

Transportation clients are sitting on more telemetry, more sensors, and more digital signal than they have ever had — and acting on less of it. Telemetry that doesn't change a decision is just storage cost.
🚂
Asset volume up
Connected vehicles, trailers, and yard assets across the THS portfolio have roughly tripled since 2020. Most of that data is logged, not acted on.
📊
Insight latency flat
Time-from-signal-to-decision for predictive maintenance, dwell, dispatch, and warranty has not meaningfully improved — still measured in days, not minutes.
🛡
OEM & ISV pressure
Geotab, Samsara, Trimble, Platform Science are climbing the value stack. Every quarter without an agentic platform is captured analytics value carriers don't get back.
FedEx, Hertz, and JB Hunt do not have a data problem. They have a decision-velocity problem.

AXLE-ASSET-01 — one chain,
top to bottom, fleet-translated.

The scenario in the architecture doc is a stamping press. Translated for fleet: a Class 8 tractor with a degrading turbocharger, two days before failure.
Scenario
National LTL fleet runs at 72–78% asset availability. Roadside breakdowns cost $2,400–$8,000/event including driver detention and missed delivery windows. Early-failure indicators sit in OEM portals nobody opens between 10pm and 6am.
Real-Time Hub ingests OEM telematics + ECU streams to Bronze
Solution
PdM agent fuses ECU signals (turbo speed, EGR, oil pressure, DPF differential), telematics duty-cycle data, and historical RUL distributions. Surfaces predicted-failure window with confidence interval and proposed bay-and-parts staging.
LEDGER carries OEM service-bulletin doctrine; Purview classifies driver / vehicle PII
Use Case
Pilot tractor cohort (e.g. 1,200 Cascadias on a single corridor). PdM agent reads AXLEML.Equipment, AXLEML.Shift, OEM telematics stream via Real-Time Hub. MCP tool catalog includes mcp.fleet-asset-rul, mcp.bay-availability, mcp.parts-stock.
