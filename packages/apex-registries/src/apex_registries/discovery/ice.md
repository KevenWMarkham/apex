# ICE — Discovery Prompts

Discovery prompts for connected-product OEM engagements (industrial controls,
consumer electronics, medical devices).

## Triggering-event probes

1. "What's your installed-base telemetry posture today — connected, partially
   connected, or aspirational?"

2. "Field-warranty PPM trend on top-3 product families — supplier-correlated,
   firmware-correlated, or installation-correlated?"

3. "Asset MTBF on flagship installed product — and what's your service-tier
   first-time-fix rate?"

4. "Dealer-allocation accuracy: when you allocate to dealer-region X, how often is
   it the right move? Channel-conflict frequency?"

5. "Parts-fill rate trend in service / aftermarket?"

## Architecture / data probes

6. "Connected-product platform — ThingWorx, Predix, AWS IoT, Azure IoT Hub,
   homegrown? Sprint 15 adapter shape varies."

7. "Dealer / channel system — Salesforce Manufacturing Cloud, in-house DMS? DealerML
   schema mapping varies."

8. "Service-management — ServiceNow CSM, Salesforce Field Service, in-house? Adapter
   choice."

9. "PLM — Teamcenter, Windchill? IP classification scope."

## Audit / governance probes

10. "Engineering data classification — controlled-unclassified, EAR / ITAR
    relevant?"

11. "Cybersecurity posture for connected products — IEC 62443 maturity, FDA
    cybersecurity (medical-device)?"

## Commercial probes

12. "Where's value-share cleanest: warranty cost reduction, parts-fill-rate uplift,
    first-time-fix improvement, asset MTBF uplift?"

13. "Wave-1 budget shape ($0.75-1.5M envelope, 8-12 weeks) for one product family +
    one channel region?"
