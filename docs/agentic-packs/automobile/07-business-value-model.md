# 07 — Business Value Model (Automobile Channel)

> _Draft — three revenue layers applied to the vehicle lifecycle. Purchase moments dominate; steady-state ownership monetizes through insurance + ongoing services._

## 1. Per-household economic envelope

### Steady-state year (no purchase event)

| Source | Annual |
|---|---|
| Subscription ($11.99 × 12) | $144 |
| AUT-01 discovery referrals (passive) | $5–15 |
| AUT-03 financing servicing share (existing loan/lease) | $5–15 |
| AUT-04 insurance ongoing commission (12% × $1,400–2,200) | $170–265 |
| AUT-05 aftermarket commerce | $30–80 |
| AUT-06 charging/fueling optimization share | $50–120 |
| AUT-07 fleet (when applicable) | $40–100 |
| **Annual GP (no purchase)** | **$440–740** |

### Purchase-event year

Add to steady-state:
| Source | Per event |
|---|---|
| AUT-02 purchase attribution (1% × $28K used / $48K new) | $280–480 |
| AUT-03 financing origination + APR-improvement share | $150–400 |
| AUT-04 insurance new-policy commission | $250–500 |
| AUT-08 resale attribution (trade-in side) | $100–300 |
| **Purchase-event add-on** | **$780–1,680** |
| **Annual GP (purchase year)** | **$1,220–2,420** |

### Blended per-household per-year

Households purchase every 3–4 years (1.8 vehicles staggered):
- 25–33% of HH-years are purchase years
- Blended: 0.30 × $1,800 + 0.70 × $590 ≈ **$540–1,000 / HH / yr**

## 2. Telco-level

20M home-internet HH × 15–25% attach × $540–1,000 = **$1.6–5.0B / yr GP**

This is one of the **top-3 Channel GP envelopes** in the marketplace, alongside Home and Travel.

## 3. Cost stack

| Cost line | Per HH / mo |
|---|---|
| Inference + integration (high — many partners) | $0.50–1.20 |
| Partner-API quota fees | $0.30–0.80 |
| Credit-pull fees (AUT-03) | $0.20–0.50 amortized |
| Insurance underwriting integration | $0.10–0.30 |
| Customer-care load (high — auto-purchase is emotional) | $2–6 |
| Compliance — state insurance + DMV automation | $0.30–0.80 |

Gross margin envelope: **65–82%** — slightly lower than other Channels due to higher integration + customer-care load, but still healthy.

## 4. CAC / churn protection

Vehicle purchase is the single highest-emotional-stakes commerce moment in the consumer's life. Successfully delivered, it creates a **multi-year retention anchor**:

| Cohort | Connectivity-line churn |
|---|---|
| Home Channel + Automobile (no purchase yet) | 5–8% (baseline benefit from Channel attach) |
| Home Channel + Automobile (1 successful purchase) | 1–3% (purchase-event moat is extraordinary) |

A successful AUT-02 purchase experience may be the single most powerful churn-reduction event in the marketplace.

## 5. Sensitivity

| Risk | P&L impact |
|---|---|
| AutoNation partnership doesn't close | -40% to purchase-stage GP; can fall back to Lithia / Penske |
| Cox Automotive opts out | -30% to discovery + valuation; harder to replace (no equivalent data infrastructure) |
| Progressive opts out | -25% to insurance GP; State Farm / Geico can substitute partially |
| New-vehicle market enters extended downturn | -20–30% to purchase-event GP (counter: longer-term ownership drives more AUT-04/05/06 revenue per HH) |
| Auto-finance regulatory tightening (CFPB action) | Variable; could cut AUT-03 commission structure 30–50% |
| EV transition accelerates faster than modelled | Net positive (AUT-06 charging-side coverage strengthens; AUT-04 insurance models shift to telematics-heavy) |

## 6. Lifetime-value lens

The most important framing: **AUT-04 + AUT-06 are recurring, AUT-02 + AUT-03 + AUT-08 are episodic.** A 10-year household relationship produces:

- 10 × subscription = $1,440
- 10 × insurance + ongoing = $2,000–3,500
- 2–3 purchase events × $1,000–1,500 = $2,000–4,500
- **10-year household LTV: $5,400–9,400 in Telco GP**

Vs Home Channel ~$3,000–5,000 / 10 yr. The Automobile Channel produces materially higher LTV per engaged subscriber.

## 7. Open questions for the financial model

- Auto-finance regulatory environment (CFPB direction)
- EV-transition pace + dealer-network response
- Insurance-data-sharing regulation per state
- Used-car-market volatility (post-COVID baseline still uncertain)
- Direct-sale (Tesla model) expansion to other OEMs
