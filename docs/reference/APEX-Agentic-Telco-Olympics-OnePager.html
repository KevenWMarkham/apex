§ 1 · The Olympics scenario · what the household actually experiences

One trip. One vault. Every Channel awake.

A four-person household — two parents, two teenagers, plus one elderly parent staying behind in the family home — travels to the Games. The household is on the marketplace's Family bundle + Travel Premium + Walmart Channel + Toyota Mobility + AutoNation Automobile + Sazerac CPG add-ons. § 3 · The three Olympic-moment wedges · customers remember these forever

Memorable moments only the marketplace can deliver.

Travel × Home wedge

The IROPS save that gets you to opening ceremony

AA flight cancelled day-of-Games. Flight Concierge auto-routes via Delta + ground-handling at host airport so one parent + tickets arrive in time. Family calendar + venue arrival window cross-referenced; loyalty number attached; rebook submitted in < 5 min. The marketplace turns a vacation-ending event into a footnote.

Eldercare continuity wedge

"Mom is OK" without a vacation-killer alarm

Grandparent's ADL drift escalates to in-state family contact, not to the travelers — quiet status surfaces for awareness. Trip continues. This is the trust-asymmetry argument made concrete: only the vault-first Telco can route this signal carefully. No Big Tech alternative has the household state machine to know who to call.

Allocation × Travel wedge

The host-city commemorative bottle

Sazerac releases a 100-bottle host-city single-cask at one licensed retailer. The customer never sees the secondary market price; the marketplace reserves a bottle before the customer leaves home, age-verified via vault, picked up two metro stops from the hotel. Cellar inventory updates. The collector parent talks about this trip for a decade.

§ 6 · Why the Olympics is the marketplace's proof point

Highest-friction household event of the four-year cycle.

Why the Olympics maxes out the marketplace

  • Multi-stakeholder, multi-Channel, multi-time-zone. Every Channel earns its keep simultaneously over a defined 16-day window.
  • Known event window. Pre-trip orchestration starts T-30d; post-trip reunification ends T+24h. Tight enough to measure cleanly.
  • Cross-border friction. Currency, language, regulation, payment, age-verification, prescription, vehicle insurance — all break in normal travel; marketplace pre-handles them.
  • Emotional stakes maximal. A missed opening ceremony, a stranded grandparent, a lost bottle of single-cask — these are the moments the household remembers permanently.
  • Demonstrates cross-Channel composition. Travel × Home × CPG × Mobility × Retail × Media all coordinate via the orchestrator. A pure-Travel app cannot do this.
  • Defensible against Big Tech. No Apple/Google/Amazon equivalent can route the eldercare-back-home signal carefully and orchestrate the allocation pickup and handle the IROPS rebook and stay quiet during opening ceremony.

Telco upside · summary

  • $170–340 / HH GP from a single Games trip (one household, one event).
  • Marketing-class moment: customer-facing wedge events drive 2–3 referrals per successful trip.
  • Partnership pipeline accelerant: every brand wants the Olympics surface; the marketplace becomes the obvious distribution path.
  • Multi-cycle compounding: same households attend FIFA World Cup (2026), Super Bowl, Coachella, Burning Man, college reunions — the trip template repeats.
  • Sponsorship arbitrage: brands pay premium for visibility during the Games window; marketplace surfaces (with disclosure) capture share that would otherwise go to TV / OOH.
  • Independence-safe: the marketplace structure stays brand-neutral throughout — Telco never competes with any partner brand.
