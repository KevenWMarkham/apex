# 06 — Strategic Partnership Map (Mobility Channel)

## 1. Anchor — Toyota

**Toyota Motor North America** with three captive entities participating:
- **Toyota Connected** (telematics + Vehicle Health Reports)
- **Toyota Financial Services** (loans, leases, refi)
- **Toyota Insurance Management Solutions** (telematics-based UBI)

Plus the **Toyota / Lexus dealer network** (~1,200 US dealers) as channel-fulfillment partners.

Archetype mix: B + C + D + E (all four).

| Toyota benefit | Telco benefit |
|---|---|
| Default-routing for Toyota vehicles in the household | $9.99 / mo subscription |
| Lower-CAC TFS refi origination | Per-refi attribution fee |
| TIMS UBI enrolment funnel | Per-policy attribution + outcome share |
| Higher recall-completion rate (NHTSA exposure reduction) | Per-recall service-attribution fee |
| Multi-Telco distribution from one MCP integration | Brand-neutral routing for non-Toyota household vehicles |

## 4. The three platform-defining partnerships for Mobility

1. **Toyota** as anchor (justified above)
2. **OnStar (GM)** as standards/E-archetype partner — GM has the most mature consumer-telematics + most experience with multi-Telco partnerships
3. **A captive-insurance partner** — either TIMS at Toyota, or a non-captive UBI carrier like Progressive — to unlock the D-archetype layer that takes the Channel from ARPU-only to LTV-driven

## 5. Conflict / overlap considerations

- **MOB-02 vs RTL-03 (Walmart Auto Care).** Both service routine maintenance. The orchestrator decides based on (a) warranty (under warranty → dealer), (b) price ($150 oil change at dealer vs $40 at TLE for non-warranty), (c) customer preference (some prefer dealer always; some prefer convenience). Both partners coexist.
- **MOB-04 vs future Finance Channel.** TFS / TIMS overlap with general-purpose loan / insurance services. The captive-OEM products win on Toyota vehicles; general-purpose carriers win on non-Toyota vehicles. The orchestrator routes by household-fleet composition.
