# The APEX Sellers Podcast · `pc-sellersguide`

A seven-episode audio companion to **Volume I of the APEX Trilogy** — the *Professional APEX-M Sellers Guide* (`/docs/book/Professional-APEX-M-Sellers-Guide.html`). Produced in the spirit of *The Acquired* podcast (Ben Gilbert + David Rosenthal): deep, narrative, two-host, story-driven, with recurring segments and "the work behind the work."

> **Format reference:** Acquired-style. Cold open → theme → narrative → APEX Facts → Adopt/Hold debate → Lessons → Carve Outs. Roughly 30 minutes per episode.

---

## The hosts

**Keven** *(the practitioner)*
Deloitte VP, Microsoft Technology & Services Practice. 22+ years on the Microsoft platform. Has been in the room for the deals being described. Plays the *Ben Gilbert* role — the operator who walks the listener through what actually happens when the framework meets the customer.

**Jordan** *(the strategist · skeptic)*
Strategy partner, Industrial Products & Services. Plays the *David Rosenthal* role — the wide-angle thinker who pushes back, asks the obvious-but-essential question, and reframes from the buyer's seat ("but the CFO is going to ask…"). Jordan keeps the show honest: when the framework over-promises, Jordan calls it.

The hosts disagree productively. They use first names, banter, and "wait, back up — let's actually define that." No corporate voice. No marketing varnish.

## Production notes

- **Scripts are speakable.** Every line is written to be read aloud. Stage directions appear in `[brackets]`. Em-dashes mark natural pause points.
- **No internal codenames in the audio.** Where the source guide says "ORCH-01" or "BRML," the script translates to the client-safe equivalent ("the warranty root-cause orchestration," "the build-record schema family").
- **Independence-safe.** No reference to ECIF in any positive context. DCIF, value-share, and direct funding only.
- **Anchor accounts are anonymised in audio.** Where Part IV names specific companies (Big Box Store, Nike, etc.), the audio version uses the *archetype* phrasing the Sellers Guide already provides ("the national big-box retailer with 400 stores"). Sellers can map back to the named accounts via the Sellers Guide.
- **Numbers are the same as the source.** ROI math, capacity sizings, and KPI envelopes match the Sellers Guide exactly. If the guide updates, the scripts update.

## How to use these scripts

1. **As a listener** — read straight through, or hand off to a narrator/AI-voice pipeline. The scripts are timed at ≈ 165 words/minute conversational pace, which is *The Acquired* norm.
2. **As a seller onboarding tool** — Episodes 1, 2, and 3 are the new-hire fast lane. A first-week seller can listen on a commute and arrive at the desk knowing the commercial arc and the four pillars.
3. **As a pursuit prep tool** — pick the industry episode (4) or anchor-account episode (5 or 6) the morning of a discovery call. The "Lessons" segment is the pre-meeting briefing.
4. **As an architecture prep tool** — Episode 3 (Four Pillars) is the platform-architecture brief; bring it to the architect conversation, not to a partner-channel meeting.
