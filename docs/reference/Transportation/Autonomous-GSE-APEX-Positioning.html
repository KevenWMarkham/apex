Reading the Slice

This is a vertical slice through the APEX 4-tier architecture, surfaced for autonomous Ground Support Equipment. Outcomes are measurable; sub-Solutions are buyer-shaped; Services are catalogued SKUs; Foundation is shared.

A typical engagement starts with the Operator sub-Solution (Wave 1), expands into OEM and Regulatory in Wave 2, and resolves into the Commercial / FaaS posture in Wave 3.
