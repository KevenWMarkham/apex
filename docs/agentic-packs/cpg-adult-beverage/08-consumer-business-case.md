# 08 — Consumer Business Case (Beverage Channel)

> _Draft — allocation wedge for collectors; cocktail concierge for everyday; tasting events for relationship-deepening._

## Wedge — Buffalo Trace Antique Collection

The BTAC drops every fall (5 bottles: Stagg, William Larue Weller, Sazerac Rye 18, Eagle Rare 17, Thomas H. Handy). MSRP is roughly $99–$129 per bottle; secondary-market price is $400–2,500+. Customers spend hours / days hunting drops at local retailers.

The Channel collapses the hunt into:
1. Watchlist + alert (set once)
2. Automatic reserved hold when a drop matches
3. One-tap purchase confirmation
4. Pickup window with age-verified pickup

Per-engaged-collector value: avoiding 5–20 hours / yr of hunting × subjective value ($25–80 / hr) = **$125–1,600 / yr in time value**, plus avoided $200–2,000 / yr in secondary-market premiums.

Against a $4.99 / mo subscription, the value math is overwhelming for the collector cohort.

## Everyday hook — cocktail concierge

For non-collectors, the everyday-utility surface is:
- "What can I make tonight?" → suggests cocktail based on cellar + weather + day
- Identifies missing ingredients → routes to BEV (spirits) + Retail (mixers, citrus, garnish)
- Tracks bottle inventory across openings → forecasts when to reorder

Per-household value: 4–8 fewer "ran out of mixers mid-recipe" trips per year × $15–25 each = $60–200 / yr.

## Tasting events — relationship deepening

Distillery tours and tasting events are emotional anchors:
- Sazerac House tour (New Orleans)
- Buffalo Trace Distillery tour (Frankfort, KY)
- Diageo Crown Royal experience (Manitoba, Canada)
- Pernod Ricard's Glenlivet (Speyside, Scotland)

Per-household value: high — these are vacation-anchor experiences. Booking + group coordination + transportation orchestration through the Channel + Travel Channel cross-collaboration.

## Adoption funnel

| Stage | Envelope |
|---|---|
| Home Channel subscribers | 100% |
| Adult-beverage household (legal-age member) | 65–75% |
| Aware of Beverage Channel | 50–65% |
| Triggered to trial (often via BEV-03 cocktail content) | 12–22% |
| Active at 90 days | 85–92% |
| Collector segment (allocation-engaged) | 2–4% of subscribers |

## What kills the Channel

- **Compliance failure in one state** — could pause the Channel nationally; legal exposure is real
- **Allocation scalper litigation** — claim Channel disadvantages walk-in customers; needs proactive narrative
- **Sazerac partnership friction** — if Sazerac decides to direct-launch, the anchor weakens
- **Underage-purchase incident** — single high-profile case damages trust irreparably; age-verification must be airtight
