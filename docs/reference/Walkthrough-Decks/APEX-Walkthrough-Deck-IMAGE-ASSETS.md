# APEX Walkthrough Deck — Image Asset List (v3)

The deck `APEX-Walkthrough-Deck-for-DMTSP-Sellers-v3.pptx` ships with **photo
placeholder boxes** in 11 of the 15 slides. Each placeholder is a styled
rectangle marked with a 📷 icon, the words "INSERT IMAGE", and a quoted
search-term hint.

**To drop in a real photo in PowerPoint:**

1. Click the placeholder rectangle.
2. Right-click → **Change Picture** → choose source:
   - **From Stock Images** (Microsoft 365 — recommended; includes royalty-free
     Unsplash-style photography licensed for commercial use)
   - **From a File** (your own / Deloitte brand library)
   - **From Online Pictures** (Bing image search with Creative Commons filter)
3. Use the suggested search term below as your query.
4. After dropping in, recommend applying **50–65% dark transparency** on
   full-bleed photos (slides 1, 9, 15) so the white overlay text stays
   legible. (Format Picture → Picture Transparency in PowerPoint.)

## Free stock-photo sources (CC0 / commercial-use-OK)

- **Microsoft 365 Stock Images** (PowerPoint → Insert → Pictures → Stock
  Images) — Unsplash photography, royalty-free, no attribution required.
  **Easiest for DMTSP.**
- **Unsplash** (https://unsplash.com) — CC0; downloadable.
- **Pexels** (https://www.pexels.com) — CC0; downloadable.
- **Pixabay** (https://pixabay.com) — CC0; downloadable.

## Workflow tip — drop all 15 photos in one session

1. Open `APEX-Walkthrough-Deck-for-DMTSP-Sellers-v3.pptx`.
2. Click View → Slide Sorter to see all 15 at a glance.
3. Double-click slide 1; replace placeholder; move on to slide 2.
4. With Microsoft 365 Stock Images, drop-in averages ~15 seconds/photo.
5. Total time to picture-complete the deck: **~5 minutes**.

After that, the deck is presentation-ready. The speaker handles narrative;
each slide is a visual anchor + 3–5 keyword chips.
